# Plan: validate the first 100 paid evaluations

Goal: prove four things with real money before scaling. A $10M+ valuation is an ambition, not a prediction; these numbers decide whether it's worth pursuing.

| Question | Metric (Admin → Overview) | "Keep going" target | "Rethink" signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Will families pay? | Checkout conversion (paid ÷ checkouts started) | ≥ 35% | < 15% |
| Will coaches deliver? | Completion (delivered ÷ notified) | ≥ 80% | < 60% |
| Is it worth it? | "Refund" rate (released holds + refunds) | ≤ 15% | > 30% |
| Do they come back? | Repeat purchase rate | ≥ 20% within 60 days | < 5% |
| Can we acquire profitably? | CAC vs. contribution per order | CAC < $25 | CAC > $60 |

Also watch: median hours to deliver (target < 72 h), profile view → paid, and which price tier sells.

## Phase 0 — Supply (weeks 1–2): 10–15 evaluators

* Recruit **former** college coaches and independent evaluators (pilot keeps active coaches off) in 3–4 sports where families
  already buy recruiting help: football, basketball, soccer, volleyball, baseball.
* Sources: your own network, LinkedIn ("former assistant coach"), club directors, camp staff.
* Pitch: "$30–$50 for 60 seconds on your phone, keep 100%, decline anything."
* Each one gets a call, identity check, eligibility basis recorded, a written agreement, and a test request to themselves.
* Turn on **Review every video before delivery** for the first ~20 orders.

## Phase 1 — First 25 orders (weeks 2–4): concierge

* Sell to warm audiences: The Athlete Market's existing audience/email list, 2–3 club teams, high-school coach partners.
* Add a "Coach Evaluations" link on theathletemarket.com (UTM-tagged).
* Personally follow up with every buyer within 24 h of delivery: "Was it worth $X? Would you buy another? From whom?"
* Fix anything that causes a coach to miss the deadline (nudge them by phone if needed and note it).

## Phase 2 — Orders 26–100 (weeks 4–10): paid acquisition tests

* Small budgets ($300–$500 per channel): Meta/Instagram parents 35–55 interested in youth sports; TikTok athlete content;
  sponsored posts with club directors and recruiting Instagram accounts.
* Log spend monthly in **Admin → Settings → Marketing spend** so CAC is computed.
* A/B one variable at a time: price tier ($30 vs. $50 for similar coaches), headline, coach featured.
* Repeat-purchase nudge: delivery page CTA "Get another coach's perspective". Test a 2-pack later.

## Unit economics to keep in mind

* Pilot (0% commission): the platform pays Stripe's fee (~$1.17–$1.75) **only on delivered orders**. Declined or expired
  requests are never charged, so they cost $0. 100 delivered orders ≈ $150 in fees. That is the price of learning.
* Remaining fee risks: an admin refund of an already-charged order (Stripe keeps the fee) and chargebacks ($15 dispute fee).
* At a 20% take rate on $40, the platform keeps $8 − ~$1.46 fee ≈ **$6.54 contribution** per delivered order before CAC.
  Paid acquisition only works if CAC is low or repeat purchases are high, which is why repeat rate matters most.

## Decision at 100

* **Scale** if conversion ≥ 35%, completion ≥ 80%, refunds ≤ 15% and repeat ≥ 20%: introduce the 20% commission for new
  coaches, recruit 50+ evaluators, invest in SEO coach pages, explore club/team bundles.
* **Iterate** if demand is strong but completion is weak: fix supply (more evaluators, shorter deadline reminders, higher coach pay).
* **Pivot** if families won't pay ≥ $30: test team/club licensing or a free-preview + paid deep-dive model.
